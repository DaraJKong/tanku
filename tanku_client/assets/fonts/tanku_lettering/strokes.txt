# Tanku Lettering: every character that the game letters, drawn in strokes of the pen after
# lettering type B of ISO 3098. The floor of the game is lettered from this file, and the font
# of the interface is made from it by `tanku_client/dev/lettering_font.py`: after a change
# here, run that script and keep the font that it writes.
#
# Coordinates are in tenths of the height of a capital: the baseline is at 0, the top of the
# capitals and of the tall small letters at 10, the top of the other small letters at 7, and
# their tails at -3. The pen is one tenth wide, two tenths part two characters, and a space is
# three wide, so that six part two words.
#
# A character is drawn by its strokes:
#
#     A 6 M0,0 L3,10 L6,0 M0.95,3.2 L5.05,3.2
#
# its width, from the middle of its leftmost line to the middle of its rightmost one, then
# `M` to lift the pen to a point, `L` to draw a line to a point, and `A` to draw an arc of an
# ellipse: its center, its two radii, and the angles in degrees, counterclockwise, from which
# and to which it goes. An arc that starts where the pen is goes on from there. A character
# that can't be written as itself here, the space and the number sign, is written U+ and its
# code.
#
# A character can be made of others:
#
#     é = e acute
#     ¼ = 1@0.5,0.5,0,5 ⁄@1,1,1.5,0 4@0.5,0.5,4.5,0 w7
#
# the first part as it is, and each other one where its numbers put it: scaled across and up,
# then moved across and up. A mark without numbers stands over the middle of the first part,
# half a pen and a pen clear of its top, or hangs under it from the baseline. Its width is
# that of the first part, unless `w` gives another.
#
# A mark is drawn as a character is, around its own origin: a mark that goes above sits on it,
# and one that goes below hangs from it.
#
#     mark acute above M-0.7,0 L0.7,1.6
